# Playbook: onboarding a new tenant

> **Stub.** Written on Day 41 (week 9) and corrected during the real onboarding on Day 42. Every time the playbook is wrong or missing something during onboarding, fix it here.

## Sections to write

1. **First conversation:** discovery questions, what counts as sensitive, where data may live, which models are acceptable.
2. **Agreements:** data handling, access, who can see what.
3. **Environment:** Terraform/AWS steps to provision or extend the environment.
4. **Tenant configuration:** name, document sources, sensitivity defaults, allowed routes/providers, API keys, rate limits.
5. **Ingestion:** loading their documents and checking the result.
6. **Access:** how they reach it (decided in an ADR on Day 39).
7. **Golden set:** writing 20–30 questions together; baseline eval run.
8. **Handover:** how they get help, how feedback is collected.
9. **Offboarding:** deleting their data and access.
